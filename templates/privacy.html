Privacy Policy

AltaScraper, operated by {{ owner }}{% if crn %} (company number {{ crn }}){% endif %}, registered in England & Wales.

In short. AltaScraper is a seller tool. If you connect your Amazon selling account to it, it reads your own listing, order, inventory and settlement data from Amazon so it can show it back to you and help you create and edit listings. It does not sell your data, does not share it with other sellers, and does not use it to train anyone's AI models.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers people who connect an Amazon selling account to AltaScraper and use the application at app.altascraper.com. If you are a customer of a seller who uses AltaScraper, this application is not the seller you bought from — contact that seller directly.

2. What the application accesses

When you authorise AltaScraper, Amazon issues it a token that lets it call Amazon's Selling Partner API on your behalf. Using that token it may read:

CategoryWhat it includesWhy
Listings & catalogue Your SKUs, ASINs, titles, bullet points, descriptions, images, product attributes, prices, handling times, and any errors Amazon reports against them To display your catalogue, create new listings, and fix ones Amazon has rejected
Inventory Quantities on hand and fulfilment settings Stock cover and restock estimates
Orders Order lines, quantities, item prices, order status, and the buyer's town and postcode where Amazon supplies them Sales reporting and profit calculation
Finances Settlement and fee data, referral and fulfilment fees, refunds To measure profit from real fees rather than estimates

Buyer personal data is the most sensitive item in that list. It is used only to display and total your own orders back to you. It is never sold, never shared with another seller, and never sent to any AI provider.

Your Amazon credentials

The token Amazon issues is stored encrypted at rest, using a key held in the hosting platform's environment configuration rather than in the application's code or its configuration file. You can revoke it at any time from Seller Central (Apps & Services → Manage Your Apps), which immediately and permanently ends this application's access without needing our involvement.

3. Who else receives data

AltaScraper is not an advertising business and has no data-broker relationships. Data reaches third parties only where the feature requires it:

RecipientWhat is sentPurpose
Amazon (Selling Partner API) Requests for your data, and listing content you choose to publish The data source, and where listings are created
Anthropic (Claude API) Product information — titles, attributes, source descriptions — when you ask the app to write or optimise listing copy. Order and buyer data is never sent. Generating listing text and images
Google (Sheets & Drive) Listing data and generated images, for accounts configured to use them Optional import/export and image storage
Hosting provider Everything the application stores, as its host Running the service

4. Where data is stored and for how long

Data is held in the application's own database on its hosting platform, kept for as long as your account is connected, so the application can show history and trends over time. When you revoke access or ask for deletion, the stored copy of your data and your Amazon token are deleted.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask for a copy of the personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. Revoking access in Seller Central stops all future processing immediately and does not require our cooperation.

6. Security

Amazon tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to a connected account's data is scoped to that account: the application refuses a request that names one account while another is open, rather than answering with whatever it has to hand. No system is perfectly secure, and this is a small operation rather than a large one — connect only accounts you are willing to connect.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially for connected sellers, the change will be reflected here and the date below updated.
